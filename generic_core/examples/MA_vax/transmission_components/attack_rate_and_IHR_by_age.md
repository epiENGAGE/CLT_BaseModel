# Attack rate and effective calibrated IHR by age group

Median [95% interval]. Attack rate = share of the age group infected over the 250-day season (N − S(T) − SV(T)) / N, including seeded infections, across the param-set stochastic runs. Effective IHR = IHR_scale|age × I_to_H_prop (unvaccinated) or × IV_to_H_prop (vaccinated), i.e. the probability that an infection is hospitalized, across the accepted MCMC draws.
