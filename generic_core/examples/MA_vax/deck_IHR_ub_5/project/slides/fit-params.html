Parameters to be fit

Transmission

  • Baseline transmission rate β
  • Humidity impact
  • Initial seed scaling
  • Time-varying multiplier m(t): 22 steps, one every 14 days

Severity and noise

  • Hospitalization-risk scaling, one per age group (0, 1-4, 5-12, 13-17, 18-49, 50-64, 65+)
  • Negative binomial dispersion φ

MCMC: 66 walkers × 4,000 steps; 1,700 burn-in, thinned every 200th → 726 posterior draws. Fit to daily admissions by age (Sep 1, 2025 – Jul 4, 2026) and end-of-season totals.

Target weights: 1 for the four youngest groups, 2 for 18-49 and 50-64, 5 for 65+, and 10 for the end-of-season totals by age. Posterior summaries are in the appendix.
