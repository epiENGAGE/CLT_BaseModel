Vaccination coverage counterfactual scenarios

Coverage targets

50, 55, 60, 65, 70%: each targeted group’s doses scaled up to reach the target; groups already above it unchanged

Fewer doses

−5, −10, −15, −20%: each targeted group’s daily doses cut by that share, same timing

Each applied to all age groups together, and to one age group at a time

Dose cuts are relative: 60% coverage becomes 54% at −10%. Targets use the scheduled coverage over the simulated season as the baseline.
