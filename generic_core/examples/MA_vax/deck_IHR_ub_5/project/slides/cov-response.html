Hospitalizations fall steadily as coverage rises

All age groups changed together. Left: season total; right: change vs. baseline; median and 95% interval. 
Overall coverage = scheduled doses summed over all age groups ÷ total population (a population-weighted average of each group’s coverage).

About 260 hospitalizations per percentage point of overall coverage between 31% and 39%, about 210 from baseline to 52%, and about 110 between 60% and 70%: the response flattens at high coverage because fewer infections are left to prevent.
