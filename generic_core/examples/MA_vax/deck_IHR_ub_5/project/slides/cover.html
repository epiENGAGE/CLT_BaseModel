in Massachusetts

Sensitivity analysis: hospitalization-risk multiplier allowed up to 5 (main analysis: up to 2)

November 3, 2026

Rémy Pasco, Susan Ptak, Shraddha Bandekar, José Luis Herrera Diestra, Kaiming Bi, Lauren Meyers

Update of the October 8 analysis: refit to the new Massachusetts data (admissions, first doses, population by county and age), now covering the season through July 4, 2026. New in this version: coverage targets of 50, 55, 60, 65 and 70%, and dose cuts of 5 to 20%. This version refits with the prior on the hospitalization-risk multipliers widened from 0.1–2.0 to 0.1–5.0; data, model and scenarios are otherwise unchanged.
