Changing one age group at a time: 18-49 matters most

% change in total hospitalizations (all ages) when only that group changes. 18-49 alone: −17% at 50% to −31% at 70%; 5-12 alone: −6% to −16%.

18-49 is the largest and least-covered group (27.6%), so raising it to a target adds the most doses. Groups already above a target (0 and 65+ at 50–60%) are unchanged, hence near-zero cells. For dose cuts, 5-12 and 18-49 each add about 4.5% at −20%.
