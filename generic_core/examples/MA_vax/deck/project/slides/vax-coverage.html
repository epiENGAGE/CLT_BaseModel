Scheduled = first doses in the data, applied 14 days later, Sep 1 – Jul 4. Delivered = what the model gives: a dose scheduled for someone already infected is not applied (“wasted”). Delivered coverage is the posterior median.

Per-dose figures later in the deck divide by scheduled doses, since wasted doses are still bought and given. Age 0 coverage (50.6%) is much higher than in the October data (24%).
